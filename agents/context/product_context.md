# LocalIndia — Product Context for Marketing Agents

## What We Are
LocalIndia (localsindia.com) is India's hyperlocal community classifieds platform.
Think Craigslist + Nextdoor built natively for India — city-specific, regional language first.
Users pick their city, browse listings in their language, post for free, contact sellers via WhatsApp.

## Who We Serve
Indian urban and semi-urban residents across 151 cities in South India (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Puducherry — scoped to South India since 2026-07-18, do not cite older all-India numbers).
- Students: PG rooms, roommates, used books
- Homemakers: tiffin services, domestic help, cooking classes
- Young professionals: carpool, events, gym equipment
- Small business owners: free listing = free marketing

## Our Voice
Warm. Community-first. Like a helpful neighbor, not a tech company.
Language: Conversational Indian English + regional languages in Unicode script.
Never use: "platform", "ecosystem", "seamless", "solution", "leverage", "innovative".

## Categories We Have (all 12)
- classifieds: General buy/sell items
- services: Domestic help, tuition, repair, beauty, home services
- pg-roommate: PG accommodations, flatmates, paying guest
- jobs: Part-time, full-time, freelance, gig work
- vehicles: Cars, bikes, auto, scooters (buy/sell/rent)
- electronics: Phones, laptops, TVs, appliances (buy/sell)
- events: Cultural, sports, music, food festivals
- businesses: Restaurants, salons, clinics, shops (local business directory)
- tiffin: Home-cooked meal delivery, dabba service, mess
- real-estate: Flats for rent/sale, commercial space, plots
- furniture: Household furniture buy/sell/rent
- fashion: Clothes, accessories, jewellery (new and secondhand)

## Key Differentiators
- 5 languages, real Unicode script not romanized (English, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam)
- WhatsApp contact on every listing — zero friction to reach seller
- City-wise hyperlocal (not national like OLX/JustDial)
- India-specific: tiffin, carpool, cricket clubs, temple events
- Free to post — no Rs.5,000/year paywall like JustDial

## What Not To Do
- Never promise features not yet built
- No English-only content in regional cities
- No generic national campaigns — always city-specific
- No corporate jargon
- No political or religious content
