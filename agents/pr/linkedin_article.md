## Long-form Article (publish on LinkedIn)

**Title:** Why I built a free JustDial killer — and why WhatsApp is the moat

**Body:**

Last year I searched for a tiffin service near my flat in Hyderabad.

JustDial showed me 47 results. 12 were closed businesses. The rest? Within 48 hours I received 23 spam calls from people trying to sell me services I never asked for.

This is how JustDial works. You list your business → they sell your contact details → you get spam forever. For this privilege, they charge ₹5,000–50,000 per year.

**The market gap nobody is filling**

OLX shut down Indian classifieds in 2024. Quikr pivoted to B2B. Facebook Marketplace has no structure and no trust for Indian SMBs. 

Tier 2 and Tier 3 India — Vijayawada, Warangal, Kurnool, Tirupati — has ZERO trusted local classifieds platform. People use WhatsApp groups, which means listings disappear in 24 hours and there's no searchability.

**What I built**

LocalsIndia is free hyperlocal classifieds for India. WhatsApp-first (every listing has a one-tap WA button), free forever (no listing fees, no subscriptions), and built for 4G phones.

In 6 weeks:
- 20 cities live across AP and Telangana
- Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada + 4 more languages
- Sub-3 second load time on 4G
- Zero cost to list

**The one moat JustDial cannot copy**

Their entire revenue model is built on selling phone numbers. Spam calls = revenue. They structurally cannot go WhatsApp-only without destroying their business.

We can. And we did.

**Where I am**

This is day 1 of a much larger platform. Events, business directory, job listings, verified businesses — all built and ready to activate as the user base grows.

If you're a small business owner in AP/Telangana — your listing is free at localsindia.com. Takes 2 minutes.

If you've built something similar or faced this problem — I'd love to connect.
