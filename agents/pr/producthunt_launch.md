## Description

LocalsIndia is India's free alternative to JustDial and OLX.

JustDial charges ₹5,000–50,000/year and sells your phone number to spammers. OLX India shut down. We built the replacement.

**What we do differently:**
- Free forever — no listing fees, ever
- WhatsApp-only contact — your number stays private
- 8 Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada + more)
- Sub-3s load on 4G — built for Tier 2/3 India
- Verified sellers badge via WhatsApp activity

**Who it's for:**
Tiffin services, PG room owners, job seekers, freelancers, small shops, tutors, auto drivers — anyone in India who needs local reach without paying for it.

Live in 20+ cities across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana today. Expanding across India.
